ICEBREAKER / PARENT ACCOUNTS

My IceBreaker — parent accounts

Planned feature: no production account service or actual email delivery exists yet.

Sign-up and access

Offer verified email registration or an approved identity provider, account recovery, MFA for privileged roles, session management, and privacy notices. A parent account does not automatically become a guardian account.

Favorites

  1. Follow one or more teams by stable team ID.
  2. Follow players by stable player ID, subject to player-profile visibility.
  3. Set report delivery preference (on certification, digest, off).
  4. Unfollow at any time and immediately stop future discretionary updates.

End-of-game report

After certification: subject names the matchup and date; the report includes final score, periods, goal and penalty summary, and permitted favorite-player totals. Corrections after certification generate a clearly labeled revised report instead of a duplicate “final”.

Notifications

Require affirmative opt-in, verification of recipient address, send deduplication, suppression/unsubscribe, bounce management, delivery audit, and appropriate policies for youth-related data.

Privacy

A favorite does not grant access to a minor's protected roster information. Guardianship must be separately verified before exposing guardian-only information.
