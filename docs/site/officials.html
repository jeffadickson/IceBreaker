ICEBREAKER / OFFICIALS & SIGNING

Officials and electronic sign-off

Planned production workflow; current prototype only has an authenticated development certification operation.

Official review checklist

  1. Open the assigned game's review invitation and authenticate as the assigned referee/authorized certifier.
  2. Inspect the exact scoresheet revision, final score, events, penalty durations and unresolved TBD entries.
  3. Request corrections when necessary; record a reason and keep previous revisions immutable.
  4. When the record is correct, explicitly select Approve & Sign.

Signature record

Retain game ID, approver's authenticated identity and role, explicit intent-to-sign text, UTC time, immutable snapshot digest, event sequence/revision, credential context, and audit event.

Policy-driven approvers

League rules determine whether one designated referee suffices or multiple signatures are required. A central override requires stricter authorization and immutable explanation.

Offline procedure

Offline approval must bind to the exact local scoresheet revision and an independently authenticated official identity; server verification remains mandatory before central certification.

Legal limitation

The approval system should not be represented as a general-purpose legally compliant e-signature service without jurisdiction-specific legal review. For waivers/contracts, integrate a mature external signing provider.
