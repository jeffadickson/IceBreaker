ICEBREAKER / LEAGUE CENTER

League Center

Browse /league.html for team standings, league schedules, player totals, and individual game detail.

Data semantics

Live or final-but-unverified games may appear with explicit status, but certified results alone contribute to official standings and season player statistics.

Standings

Display games played, wins, losses, ties, goals for/against, goal differential, and points. Rule sets for standings and tiebreakers must be league-configurable and pinned by season/division.

Schedule

Filter by team, date and game status. Include rink/venue, local time zone, and postponement/cancellation states. Future production features include bracket dependencies and ICS calendar subscriptions.

Players and roster stability

Reference a stable player ID. A number change, team transfer or corrected score must not create a duplicate player record. Publication of youth profile information follows league-approved privacy settings.

Corrections

Previously certified games require a new approved revision and recalculation, not in-place record mutation.

Current local prototype uses seeded fictional teams and results for demonstrations. It is not connected to a real hosted league.
