ICEBREAKER / MERCY RULES

Mercy-rule configuration

IceBreaker treats the clock policy and arena score-display cap as independently configurable.

Example

Official scoreArena score (5-goal differential cap)Parent Live
6–26–26–2
9–27–29–2
10–38–310–3

Settings

  • Goal-differential activation threshold, earliest eligible period.
  • Latched activation versus dynamic re-evaluation after lead decreases.
  • Clock run-through for ordinary stoppages, with mandatory exception list.
  • Display cap and restoration behavior after score changes.

Operator guidance

Never enter a capped arena score into the official event journal. The scorekeeper and referees always see actual totals. A rule change or override creates an auditable event.
