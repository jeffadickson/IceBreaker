ICEBREAKER / PARENT LIVE TRACKER

Live Game Tracker

Parents and spectators use /live.html for a read-only, mobile-friendly timeline with the real full score.

What appears

  • Goals and attributed scorer/assists; TBD remains explicit until resolved.
  • Penalties with period/time, team and permitted identifying information.
  • Period boundaries, current clock, game status, and correction indicators.
  • Connectivity/reconnection and provisional status indicators.

Mercy-rule policy

Mercy-rule score caps only affect the rink's public arena scoreboard. Parent Live shows all approved goals and the full official score. The event stream may reveal the complete margin, intentionally.

Corrections and reconnects

Corrections update the earlier goal attribution without creating an extra goal. Clients use stable event IDs and revision numbers to prevent duplicates during reconnect. A stream disruption must be clearly reported, not presented as live.

Privacy

This is a public-facing projection, not raw event-journal access. Private operator actions, administrative comments, sensitive identity records, and unapproved personal details must never appear.
