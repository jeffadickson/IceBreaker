# IceBreaker v2 — game-operations acceptance requirements

Status: **partially implemented**. See test references and outstanding release blockers.

- **GAME-201 P0:** Goals shall increment the team's shots-on-goal total by exactly one, including on journal replay. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-202 P0:** Non-goal shots shall be persisted as distinct `SHOT_RECORDED` journal events. Test: `penalty-clock.test.js`.
- **GAME-203 P0:** Penalty timers shall advance exclusively with elapsed *game clock* time, remain unchanged on clock `SET`, and recover paused after process restarts. Tests: `penalty-clock.test.js`, `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-204 P0:** A power-play goal shall release the earliest eligible minor penalty; a major and a shorthanded goal shall not cause early release. Test: `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-205 P0:** An active double minor shall lose no more than one two-minute segment after a qualifying power-play goal. Test: `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-206 P0:** Official events and corrections shall remain append-only, with an operator audit log and text mirror. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-207 P0:** End-of-game report shall be clearly marked UNOFFICIAL, include team score, both shot totals, goal scorers/assists, penalty history, and period breakdowns. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-208 P0:** Public parent tracker must show the full score; venue mercy display can be capped independently. Test: `penalty-clock.test.js`.

## Open release blockers

1. Delayed penalties and queued manpower reduction, multiple coincidental groups, penalty-served-by-substitute and goalkeeper-specific rule variants.
2. Full video/browser manual confirmation and touchscreen usability validation.
3. Referee electronic certification and supported correction-after-certification workflow.
4. Native Avalonia integration and hardware switch/display protocol tests.
5. Formal ruleset versioning (league-specific penalty and overtime policies) and conformance scenarios.

## Advanced rule-engine increment (2026-10-12)

- **GAME-209 P0:** When a team has reached its configured maximum concurrent manpower-reducing penalties, the next penalty shall be queued without consuming penalty service time until a slot becomes available. Test: `advanced-penalties-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-210 P0:** A delayed penalty shall not run or reduce manpower before the whistle creates the assessed penalty, and may be explicitly waved. Test: `advanced-penalties-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-211 P0:** The offender and player serving the penalty shall be stored as distinct fields in the official event; player substitution shall never silently change offender attribution. Test: `advanced-penalties-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-212 P0:** Each league shall supply a validated, versioned ruleset with minimum skaters, penalty durations, and early-termination policy. The engine now supports policy injection; durable version selection and adoption remains open.
- **GAME-213 P0:** Every delayed assessment, waiver, whistle, substitute, and correction shall be an immutable journal event with a durable outbox record. Persistence and API integration require additional tests.
- **GAME-214 P0:** Referee approval must bind the exact final event revision and immutable scoresheet hash; no official result shall publish until policy-authorized signoff. Not yet implemented.
- **GAME-215 P0:** Game operations shall be verified with browser usability, disconnect/reconnect, crash recovery, and hardware clock tests. Not yet complete.

### Rule limitations still requiring validation

The replayable penalty projection now understands queueing, delayed/waved calls, optional substitute identity, and configurable minimum skaters. This is **not yet a league-certified officiating engine**. Explicit coincidental flags do not independently adjudicate matched coincidental groups; goalkeeper/bench penalties, delayed-goal washout under specific rulebooks, start-of-period penalty carryover, manpower replenishment nuances, and official league rulebook conformance still need fixtures. Offline referee signature security and correction after certification remain future work.
