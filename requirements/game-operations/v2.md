# IceBreaker v2 — game-operations acceptance requirements

Status: **partially implemented**. See test references and outstanding release blockers.

- **GAME-201 P0:** Goals shall increment the team's shots-on-goal total by exactly one, including on journal replay. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-202 P0:** Non-goal shots shall be persisted as distinct `SHOT_RECORDED` journal events. Test: `penalty-clock.test.js`.
- **GAME-203 P0:** Penalty timers shall advance exclusively with elapsed *game clock* time, remain unchanged on clock `SET`, and recover paused after process restarts. Tests: `penalty-clock.test.js`, `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-204 P0:** A power-play goal shall release the earliest eligible minor penalty; a major and a shorthanded goal shall not cause early release. Test: `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-205 P0:** An active double minor shall lose no more than one two-minute segment after a qualifying power-play goal. Test: `penalty-scenarios-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-206 P0:** Official events and corrections shall remain append-only, with an operator audit log and text mirror. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-207 P0:** End-of-game report shall be clearly marked UNOFFICIAL, include team score, both shot totals, goal scorers/assists, penalty history, and period breakdowns. Test: `game-operations-v2.test.js`.
- **GAME-208 P0:** Public parent tracker must show the full score; venue mercy display can be capped independently. Test: `penalty-clock.test.js`.

## Open release blockers

1. Delayed penalties and queued manpower reduction, multiple coincidental groups, penalty-served-by-substitute and goalkeeper-specific rule variants.
2. Full video/browser manual confirmation and touchscreen usability validation.
3. Referee electronic certification and supported correction-after-certification workflow.
4. Native Avalonia integration and hardware switch/display protocol tests.
5. Formal ruleset versioning (league-specific penalty and overtime policies) and conformance scenarios.
